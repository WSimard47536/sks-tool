<!-- What the project's terms mean. -->

## Hold-up capacitor

The capacitor that keeps the logger powered after a power loss, long enough to close its log file.

## Log file

A file on the logger's non-volatile memory holding the samples of one power-on session.

## Power-loss signal

The discrete input that tells the logger its main supply has failed.

## Sample

One reading of one sensor channel, with its timestamp.
